Neck Pain Is More Than Just a Stiff Neck

Neck pain is the second most common condition treated by chiropractors. In today's world of smartphones, desk jobs, and long commutes, cervical spine problems are increasingly common — especially here in the Miami area. If you're waking up with a stiff neck, experiencing sharp pain when you turn your head, or feeling tension that radiates into your shoulders, chiropractic care can help.

Common Causes of Neck Pain We Treat

  • Tech neck / forward head posture — prolonged phone and computer use shifts the head forward, straining the cervical spine
  • Muscle strain and tension — tight neck muscles from stress, poor sleep position, or overuse
  • Cervical disc herniation — a bulging or ruptured disc pressing on cervical nerves
  • Cervical facet syndrome — irritation of the small joints between vertebrae
  • Whiplash from auto accidents — rapid flexion-extension injury to neck ligaments and muscles
  • Degenerative joint disease — arthritis-related breakdown of cervical joints

How We Treat Neck Pain

Our chiropractor performs a thorough evaluation of your cervical spine, posture, and range of motion before developing your personalized care plan. Treatment may include:

  • Gentle cervical adjustments to restore proper alignment
  • Soft tissue therapy to release tight muscles and fascia
  • Traction therapy for disc-related nerve compression
  • Therapeutic exercises to strengthen neck stabilizers
  • Postural retraining and ergonomic recommendations

Relief Without Pain Medication

Many patients come to us after relying on ibuprofen, muscle relaxers, or other medications for months or years — only to find that the pain keeps coming back. That's because medication doesn't fix the structural problem. Chiropractic care corrects the underlying spinal dysfunction, giving you lasting relief rather than temporary masking of symptoms.
