Don't Wait After a Car Accident

If you've been in a car accident in Cutler Bay or anywhere in South Miami-Dade County, seeking chiropractic care quickly is critical — even if you feel "okay" right after the crash. Adrenaline and inflammation can mask serious soft tissue injuries for 24–72 hours. Delaying treatment can also jeopardize your personal injury claim.

Our chiropractic office specializes in documenting and treating auto accident injuries, and we work directly with Florida auto insurance PIP coverage so there's often no out-of-pocket cost to you.

Auto Injuries We Treat

  • Whiplash — rapid back-and-forth neck movement causing ligament and muscle tears
  • Cervical strain / sprain — neck injuries from sudden impact
  • Lower back injuries — lumbar strain, disc herniation from accident forces
  • Shoulder and arm pain — from bracing for impact or seatbelt injury
  • Headaches — cervicogenic headaches following neck trauma
  • Soft tissue injuries — muscle, ligament, and tendon damage throughout the spine

Why See a Chiropractor After a Car Accident?

Chiropractic care is one of the most effective treatments for whiplash and other soft tissue injuries because it directly addresses the spinal misalignment caused by the crash. Unlike pain medication, chiropractic adjustments restore proper joint mechanics, reduce inflammation at the source, and prevent chronic pain from developing.

Studies show that patients who receive chiropractic care after auto accidents recover faster and with fewer long-term complications than those who only receive medication.

We Work With Auto Insurance (PIP)

Florida's Personal Injury Protection (PIP) insurance covers chiropractic care after an auto accident — up to $10,000 for emergency services if you seek treatment within 14 days. Our team handles the insurance paperwork and billing so you can focus entirely on your recovery.

We also coordinate with personal injury attorneys if you are pursuing a legal claim. Proper medical documentation from a chiropractor is essential to a successful case.
