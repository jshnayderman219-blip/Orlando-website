What Is a Chiropractic Adjustment?

A chiropractic adjustment (also called spinal manipulation) is a precise, controlled force applied to a spinal joint that has become restricted in its movement due to injury, poor posture, or chronic stress. When a joint is restricted, it creates inflammation, muscle tension, and nerve irritation that leads to pain.

During an adjustment, the chiropractor restores normal motion to the joint, which reduces inflammation and relieves pressure on the surrounding nerves and muscles. The result is less pain and better function.

Is It Safe?

Chiropractic adjustments are among the safest non-invasive healthcare treatments available. Serious adverse events are extremely rare — far rarer than the risks associated with surgery or long-term use of pain medications such as NSAIDs or opioids. Most patients experience immediate relief and feel much better after their first adjustment.

Adjustment Techniques We Use

We are trained in multiple adjustment techniques so we can always find the safest and most effective approach for your specific condition and comfort level:

  • Diversified technique — the most common form; a hands-on high-velocity, low-amplitude thrust to restore joint motion
  • Gonstead technique — a highly specific, evidence-based approach using detailed spinal analysis
  • Thompson Drop technique — uses a specialized table with drop pieces for a gentler adjustment
  • Activator method — a small, spring-loaded instrument used for a very gentle, low-force adjustment; ideal for elderly or sensitive patients
  • Flexion-distraction — a gentle, rhythmic pumping motion used for disc herniations and sciatica
  • Webster technique — specific sacral adjustment for pregnant patients

What to Expect During Your First Adjustment

Your first visit begins with a thorough consultation and physical examination. We'll review your health history, identify areas of spinal dysfunction, and determine which technique is best for you. If X-rays are needed, we'll take them on-site before proceeding with treatment.

Many patients experience immediate relief after their first adjustment. It's common to feel a sense of lightness, improved range of motion, and reduced pain right away. Some patients experience mild soreness for 24 hours — similar to starting a new exercise routine — which is normal and temporary.

Regular Adjustments for Optimal Health

While many patients come to us in acute pain, regular chiropractic adjustments offer significant benefits even for healthy individuals — improved posture, better nervous system function, faster recovery from physical activity, and prevention of future injuries. Many of our Cutler Bay patients make chiropractic a regular part of their wellness routine.
