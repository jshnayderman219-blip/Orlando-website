Chiropractic Care Is Safe During Pregnancy

Many women are surprised to learn that chiropractic care is not only safe during pregnancy but is highly recommended by OBs, midwives, and doulas. The American Pregnancy Association supports chiropractic care as a safe, effective option for managing pregnancy-related discomfort throughout all trimesters.

Our chiropractor uses special pregnancy pillows and modified techniques that accommodate your growing belly at every stage of pregnancy, making adjustments comfortable and completely safe for you and your baby.

Why Pregnancy Causes So Much Pain

During pregnancy, your body produces relaxin — a hormone that loosens the ligaments of the pelvis to prepare for childbirth. This, combined with the rapidly changing center of gravity as your belly grows, places tremendous stress on the spine and pelvis. The result is often:

  • Lower back pain — extremely common, affecting up to 70% of pregnant women
  • Pelvic girdle pain — discomfort in the pelvis, hips, and pubic area from joint instability
  • Sciatica in pregnancy — sciatic nerve compression from pelvic changes and the growing uterus
  • Round ligament pain — sharp pain in the lower abdomen and groin
  • Upper back and rib pain — from the rib cage expanding and postural changes
  • Neck and headache pain — from postural strain in the upper body

Benefits of Prenatal Chiropractic

  • Significant reduction in lower back and pelvic pain
  • Improved pelvic alignment for a healthier labor position
  • May help with optimal fetal positioning (reducing breech presentations)
  • Reduced labor and delivery time reported by many patients
  • Better sleep and overall comfort during pregnancy
  • Drug-free pain management — safer for your baby than medication

Webster Technique

Our chiropractor is trained in the Webster Technique, a specific sacral analysis and chiropractic adjustment designed to reduce the effects of sacral/SI joint dysfunction and optimize pelvic alignment during pregnancy. This technique is widely used to improve comfort and support ideal fetal positioning in the third trimester.
