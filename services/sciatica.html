What Is Sciatica?

Sciatica is not a diagnosis in itself — it's a set of symptoms caused by irritation or compression of the sciatic nerve, the longest nerve in the human body. This nerve runs from your lower back, through your buttock, and down each leg. When it becomes irritated or pinched, the result is often sharp, burning, or shooting pain that radiates from the lower back down one or both legs.

Many sciatica sufferers in Cutler Bay also experience numbness, tingling, or muscle weakness in the affected leg — making it difficult to walk, sit, or sleep comfortably.

Common Causes of Sciatica

  • Herniated or bulging lumbar disc — the most common cause; disc material presses on the sciatic nerve root
  • Lumbar spinal stenosis — narrowing of the spinal canal that compresses nerve tissue
  • Piriformis syndrome — the piriformis muscle in the buttock irritates the sciatic nerve
  • Spondylolisthesis — a vertebra slips forward over the one below, pinching the nerve
  • Degenerative disc disease — worn-down discs reducing the space for nerve roots

How Chiropractic Treats Sciatica
