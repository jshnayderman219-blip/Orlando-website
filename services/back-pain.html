Back Pain Is the #1 Reason Patients Visit Our Cutler Bay Office

Back pain affects millions of Americans — and if you're in Cutler Bay, FL, you don't have to just live with it. Whether you're dealing with a sudden onset of lower back pain or chronic aching that's been building for months, our chiropractic team can help you find lasting relief.

Unlike pain medications that mask symptoms, chiropractic care addresses the root structural cause of your pain — misaligned vertebrae, compressed discs, and irritated nerves — so you get real, lasting results.

Common Types of Back Pain We Treat

  • Lower back pain (lumbar) — the most common type, often caused by muscle strain, poor posture, or disc issues
  • Upper back pain (thoracic) — often related to postural stress, muscle tension, or rib joint dysfunction
  • Herniated or bulging discs — disc material pressing on spinal nerves causing pain, numbness, or tingling
  • Degenerative disc disease — age-related wear on spinal discs causing chronic pain and stiffness
  • Muscle spasms — painful, involuntary muscle contractions often triggered by spinal misalignment
  • Facet joint syndrome — arthritis or irritation of the small joints of the spine

What to Expect at Your First Visit

Your first appointment starts with a comprehensive consultation where we listen to your history, identify the onset and pattern of your pain, and assess your posture and spinal alignment. We may take digital X-rays if indicated. Then we'll create a personalized treatment plan designed to get you pain-free as quickly as possible.

Our Chiropractic Approach to Back Pain

Depending on your diagnosis, your treatment plan may include:

  • Precise spinal adjustments to restore alignment and reduce nerve pressure
  • Soft tissue therapy and myofascial release
  • Therapeutic stretching and corrective exercises
  • Posture correction guidance
  • Lifestyle and ergonomic advice specific to your work and daily routine

Why Choose Chiropractic for Back Pain?

Research consistently shows that chiropractic care is one of the most effective treatments for both acute and chronic low back pain — often outperforming pain medication, steroid injections, and even surgery in terms of long-term outcomes. It's safe, non-invasive, and has minimal side effects.

The American College of Physicians now recommends chiropractic care as a first-line treatment before trying prescription drugs for low back pain.
