Most Headaches Have a Spinal Component

Research shows that the majority of recurring headaches — including tension headaches and many migraines — originate from problems in the cervical spine (neck) and its surrounding muscles. This type is called a cervicogenic headache. When the vertebrae of the upper neck are misaligned, they irritate the nerves and muscles that refer pain into the head.

Chiropractic care is uniquely positioned to treat these headaches because it directly corrects the spinal dysfunction at the root of the problem.

Types of Headaches We Treat

  • Tension headaches — the most common type; a band-like pressure around the head, often from neck and shoulder muscle tension
  • Cervicogenic headaches — pain originating from the upper cervical spine, often one-sided and radiating from neck to head
  • Migraine headaches — severe, often one-sided headaches with sensitivity to light and sound; many have a cervical trigger
  • Post-accident headaches — chronic headaches following whiplash or concussion injury
  • Cluster headaches — intense headaches occurring in cyclical patterns

Chiropractic Treatment for Headaches

After a thorough evaluation to identify your headache type and triggers, we create a personalized treatment plan that may include:

  • Upper cervical adjustments to correct joint dysfunction that triggers head pain
  • Soft tissue release for tight suboccipital and cervical muscles
  • Postural correction to address forward head posture (a major headache driver)
  • Lifestyle recommendations including hydration, sleep position, and stress management
  • Ergonomic guidance for desk workers and frequent phone/screen users

Break the Pain Medication Cycle

Ironically, overusing pain relievers for headaches can cause "rebound headaches" — a cycle where the medication itself becomes a trigger. Chiropractic care gives you a sustainable, drug-free path to fewer headaches and reduced dependence on over-the-counter medications.
